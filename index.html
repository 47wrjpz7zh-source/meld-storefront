// How It Works

Coverage in under 30 seconds

Three steps. No mirror gymnastics. No salon appointment.

I

Part to the thinning area

On dry hair, part or comb so the thinning area is exposed. Any length works. No prep product needed.

II

Pull, dab, apply.

Pull the applicator off the bottom of the jar. Dab the foam pad into the powder, then press it onto your scalp at the thinning area. One press lays down a full dose. No shaking, no mess.

III

Walk out. No one knows.

Long-lasting, sweat-resistant hold survives the gym and the commute. Removes cleanly with any shampoo. No residue, no buildup.

We'd rather qualify you honestly than sell to the wrong customer.

MELD works if you:

  • ✓ Have thinning hair with some coverage remaining (Norwood 1–3 range)
  • ✓ Want coverage that looks and feels like your own hair
  • ✓ Are active: gym, sport, outdoor lifestyle
  • ✓ Want to apply at home in under 30 seconds, no salon
