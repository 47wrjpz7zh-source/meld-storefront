MELD vs Hairo at a glance

MELD and Hairo are both fine-powder concealers you press onto thinning areas, and both create thicker-looking, fuller-looking hair that washes out with shampoo. The differences are in the details. MELD comes in four shades with a sweat-resistant, long-lasting hold it calls Meldfuse™, a 60-day money-back guarantee, a Free Shade Swap, and free US shipping, and it ships in a plain unbranded box. Hairo is a mineral-powder concealer that markets a long-hold claim under its own technology name, comes in nine shades, prices around GBP 29 per jar, and offers a 60-day return window in which the customer pays return shipping. Hairo is a newer brand, launched around 2025. Both are legitimate products; the right one depends on shade range, the guarantee terms, and which market you are buying from.

Meldfuse™ MELD's hold, named

Meldfuse™ is our name for the way MELD's fine micro-powder clings to and blends with your existing hair for a long-lasting, sweat-resistant hold. It is not an ingredient and not a chemical bond: it is the behavior of a finely milled powder pressed into the right place. We name it because the hold is the part that matters most in everyday use.

How MELD and Hairo are applied

Both MELD and Hairo are applied the same basic way, because both are press-on powders rather than shake-on fibers. You start with dry, styled hair, load the applicator with powder, press it onto the thinning area, and pat to blend. With MELD, the routine takes seconds: press the built-in applicator into the powder, dab it onto the thin spot, feather the edges with a fingertip, and check in daylight. Build coverage in light layers rather than one heavy dose, and work from the surrounding hair inward so the coverage fades in instead of stopping at a hard line. To remove either product, wash with any shampoo. The application feel is similar between the two, so a comfortable Hairo user will find MELD familiar from the first try.

  1. Start dry and styled. Apply to dry hair after styling, the same as you would with Hairo.
  2. Press and dab. Load the built-in applicator and press the powder onto the thinning area.
  3. Build in light layers. Add a little, check, then add more. Light layers look the most natural.
  4. Feather and check. Pat the edges to blend, then check in daylight and touch up if needed.
  5. Wash out at night. Any shampoo removes it completely, no residue or buildup.
